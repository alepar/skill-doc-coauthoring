scope: passage

Digest entries: X04, X07; keep K9, K10.
Hunt:
- [strong] Negative parallelism whose first half nobody claimed: "not X but Y", "not just/only/merely X but (also) Y", "it's not X, it's Y", "X rather than (merely) Y", "less like X and more like Y".
- [strong] The split two-sentence form: "This isn't about X. It's about Y."; "No X. No Y. Just Z."
- [weak] Mirrored-clause aphoristic enders ("The tool is simple. The discipline is not.").
- [strong] Throat-clearing pivots: "Here's the thing", "Here's where it gets interesting", "The real question is", "Let's dive in", "Let's break it down".
- [weak] Colon reveals whose left side carries no content ("The result:", "The catch?", "The kicker:").
- [weak] Self-posed "Question? Answer." beats.
- [weak] Meta-signposting that announces structure instead of delivering it ("Four caveats belong at the front").

Fix: state the positive claim directly and delete the run-up. Keep a contrast only if it corrects a belief the reader plausibly holds or both halves carry information (K9). Deletion test for colon reveals: remove the left side; if nothing is lost, it goes.

## claude
Weight "rather than merely" and "less like a _ and more like" (both 100×+ the human rate), binary aphoristic enders, and validate-then-refine ("X is correct, and it can be made precise").

## openai
Weight "the X is not simply", "X, not Y" introducing an alternative nobody raised, "This isn't about X. It's about Y.", and "Question? Answer." beats.

## gemini
Weight "is not just a X, it is"; practitioners report it survives suppression prompts.
