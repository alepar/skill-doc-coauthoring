Digest entries: X17, X20; keep K17.
Hunt:
- [weak] Category nouns where a specific member is expected ("various stakeholders", "a range of factors", "multiple teams").
- [strong] Praise or evaluation with no supporting fact.
- [strong] Sweeping openers ("In today's fast-paced world", "In an era of…").
- [weak] Paragraphs that answer a neighbouring question instead of the one the section is about.
- [strong] Templated specifics: default names (Emily, Sarah, Elara, Kael), "Dr. X, a researcher at Y", scene-setting openers ("On a drab November morning…").
- [weak] Symmetric coverage: every option gets equal space and none is ranked, when the text's purpose is to choose.
- [weak] Swap test failure: the paragraph would still read fine if the topic noun were swapped for another.

Fix: cut the sentence; narrow it to what the text actually supports; or move up a specific that already exists elsewhere in the document. If none exists, insert `[AUTHOR: specify X]` and never fill the gap yourself (R01).
Keep: marketing or overview copy whose genre is general (K17); deliberate abstraction in a summary whose specifics follow.
