Digest entries: X03, X05, X10; keep K4, K11, K13.
Hunt:
- [strong when 2+ per paragraph] Sentence-final evaluative -ing riders: ", highlighting…", ", underscoring…", ", ensuring…", ", reflecting…", ", cementing…".
- [weak] Stacked participial clauses.
- [weak] Nominalization clusters with weak verbs ("the implementation of the enhancement of").
- [weak] Rhetorical triads of near-synonyms or with an empty third item; back-to-back triads are strong.
- [weak] Doubled descriptors ("empathy and community", "clear and concise").
- [weak] Long "and"-chained sentences with little internal punctuation.
- [weak] Invented hyphenated compound labels ("exact-head checks", "trust-first posture") used as if they were established terms.

Fix: give the sentence a finite verb and a named subject; use is/are/has; cut a triad to its informative items; split and-chains at the logical seam; name a concept plainly instead of coining a label.
Keep: literal three-item enumerations; agentless passives where the agent is unknown or irrelevant; terms of art that look like nominalizations.
