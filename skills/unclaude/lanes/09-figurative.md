Digest entries: X16, C08; keep K22.
Hunt:
- [strong when 2+ per paragraph] Metaphor in place of a literal statement in expository text: "a dial worth turning", "earns its keep", "the risk lives somewhere specific", "the shape of the problem", "the physics of", "sits upstream of".
- [weak] Personified abstractions ("the decision emerges", "the data whispers").
- [weak] Simile density: more than one simile per paragraph of expository prose.
- [weak] "The [noun] of [adj] [noun]" templates ("a sea of unspoken expectations").
- [strong] Stock emotional clichés: hung in the air, a sense of unease, pit of her stomach, voice barely above a whisper.
- [strong] Fiction slop: flickered, shimmered, default names Elara and Kael.
- [weak] Pseudo-reflective framing: "the question I keep coming back to", "sit with", "worth sitting with".

Fix: say it literally; replace the figure with the plain claim it stands for. Keep at most one apt figure per explanation. Never replace one figure with another (R08).
Keep: figurative language in fiction and essays where it is the register; domain-standard metaphors (bottleneck, load-bearing wall, memory leak); a single apt metaphor (K22).
