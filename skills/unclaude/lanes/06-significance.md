Digest entries: X11, C02; keep K8, K17.
Hunt:
- [strong] Significance flags: a testament to, plays a pivotal/crucial role, underscores the importance, reflects broader trends, setting the stage for, evolving landscape, indelible mark, the stakes are high.
- [weak] "Despite its [praise], X faces challenges" paragraphs.
- [weak] Puffery: boasts, vibrant, rich, nestled, in the heart of, groundbreaking, renowned, diverse array.
- [weak] Pseudo-aphorisms: "at its core", "what really matters".
- [strong] Self-ranking and totalizing superlatives with no criterion: "the single most", "arguably the most", "every single", "the whole game", "the only thing that matters", "the deepest point".
- [strong] Endings that summarize, uplift, moralize, or zoom out to legacy, community or the world.

Fix: keep the fact and drop the significance claim; replace the adjective with the fact behind it; give a superlative its criterion or delete it; end on the last concrete fact. Never add a new significance claim to replace one.
Keep: sourced, specific significance claims; marketing copy written as marketing (K17); superlatives that are literally true (K8).

## claude
Weight "matters because" / "matters more than", "single most", "every single", and Opus 5.5 helpfulness phrasing ("can help you", "is especially helpful"). Claude tends to overstate; this lane is a priority for Claude text.

## openai
Weight GPT-4o-era puffery (vibrant, testament, tapestry) and GPT-6 "Bottom Line:" framing.

## gemini
Weight intensifier stacking (incredibly, absolutely).
