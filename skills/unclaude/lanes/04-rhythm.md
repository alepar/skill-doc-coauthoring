Digest entries: X06, X10; keep K3, K14.
Hunt:
- [weak] Em-dash rate per 1,000 words outside code and quotations above about 4 (heuristic), or above the writer's sample rate when a sample is supplied. Report the rate once as a document finding, then the worst 3–5 instances.
- [strong] Two em dashes in one sentence used as an aside pair plus a third elsewhere in the paragraph; "it's not X — it's Y" fusions.
- [weak] Semicolon cadence: semicolons joining clauses at a rate that reads as a dash substitute.
- [weak] Runs of 5+ consecutive sentences of similar length (heuristic) with no question, parenthetical or varied punctuation.
- [strong] Staccato fragment runs and stacked one-sentence paragraphs used for drama.
- [weak] Long under-punctuated sentences joined by "and".

Fix: choose punctuation from the logical relation between clauses (period, comma, colon, parentheses), never one stock substitute for the dash. Vary rhythm by changing what sentences do, never by chopping them into fragments.
Keep: dashes at or below the writer's own rate; dashes in code, ranges and compound modifiers; a single dash.
